Helping Parents Make Disciples at Home

About Everything Doulos Store

Everything Doulos Store is a faith-based store created to provide parents with meaningful, practical and intentional resources to help nurture their children's faith.

We believe discipleship begins at home.

We know parenting comes with busy days, full schedules, bedtime routines, big emotions and countless responsibilities. In the middle of it all, we want to help parents create simple moments where faith becomes part of everyday family life.

Our resources are designed to help parents teach, speak, pray, read, reflect and grow in faith with their children. From Scripture cards to story books and journals, Everything Doulos Store exists to put practical discipleship tools into the hands of parents.

Because raising children in the knowledge and love of God isn't just about what we tell them. It's about what we intentionally build into their everyday lives.

Why "Doulos"?

Doulos is a Greek word associated with a servant.

At Everything Doulos Store, our heart is to serve families by creating resources that encourage and equip parents as they embrace the call to disciple their children.

We want to serve parents with resources that make it easier to bring faith into everyday family life.

Our Vision

Raising a Generation Rooted in God's Word

Our vision is to see families intentionally embrace discipleship at home and raise children who know God's Word, recognize His love and grow in faith from their earliest years.

We envision homes where Scripture isn't reserved for Sunday mornings, but becomes part of everyday conversations, prayers, stories, questions and moments of connection.

We want to see a generation of children growing up with God's Word spoken over them, taught to them and lived out before them.

Our Mission

Equipping Parents to Disciple With Intention

Our mission is to create faith-based resources that make family discipleship practical, intentional and meaningful.

We equip parents with thoughtfully created tools that help them speak Scripture over their children, create meaningful faith conversations at home, introduce children to biblical truths in age-appropriate ways, build intentional moments of prayer and reflection into everyday life, and make discipleship a natural part of family life.

Everything Doulos Store exists to serve parents as they raise children who know, love and follow God.
